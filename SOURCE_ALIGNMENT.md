## High-value corrections/checks reflected in the bank

- Commercial paper is a **money-market** instrument, not a capital-market instrument.
- Federal funds are very short-term interbank loans.
- Negotiable CDs are transferable/negotiable time-deposit instruments.
- A repo is a collateralized short-term financing arrangement structured as a sale and repurchase of securities; it is not corporate debt issued by a large firm.
- In a dealer market, the bid is normally below the ask; the spread compensates the dealer.
- Bankers' acceptances are short-term trade-finance instruments, not long-term bank funding.
- Zero-coupon bonds make no periodic coupon payments.
- Newly issued stock is sold in the primary market and provides equity financing to the issuer.
- Money-market problems distinguish discount yield, bond-equivalent yield and effective annual return rather than treating quoted yields as interchangeable.
- TVM problems enforce timeline consistency and matching the rate period to the cash-flow period.
- Firm-performance problems distinguish margins, turnovers, liquidity and leverage ratios and explicitly explain denominator choice.

## Exercise design

The 600-question bank combines:

1. instructor/tutorial-linked checks based on the supplied exercises;
2. numerical variants generated from the same formula structures;
3. conceptual rapid-review variants for retrieval practice.

Generated variants are labeled as generated; they are not presented as verbatim questions from the supplied sources.
